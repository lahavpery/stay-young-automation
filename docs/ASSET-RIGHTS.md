# Original artwork

All twelve final PNG designs were drawn for this project from original geometric shapes with Pillow and the installed DejaVu Sans font. They depict skincare bottles, mirrors, a colorful food plate, a walking shoe, a water glass, a book, botanical forms, the sun and moon. No stock photos, third-party likenesses, testimonials, medical illustrations or before/after images were used. No image service/subscription is involved.

The images may be used commercially with these marketing posts. DejaVu permits rendered images; no font files are redistributed. SVG files are alternate original botanical templates, not exact editable representations of the final PNG designs. The included scripts/build-assets.py reproduces the final PNG assets when Pillow and DejaVu are installed; rendering is not needed by the cloud publisher because PNGs are committed assets.
